How it works

  1. 1

    Ask

    SoTeach starts by asking you to explain what you already know about the topic — it never assumes.

  2. 2

    Find the gap

    From your own words, it pinpoints the exact thing you don’t understand yet.

  3. 3

    Teach & verify

    It teaches only that gap, checks you can use it on a new question, then confirms you’ve truly got it.
